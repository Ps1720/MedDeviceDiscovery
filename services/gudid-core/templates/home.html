How it works

Three steps. Thirty seconds.

01

Scan the UDI

Point the scanner at the device package, or paste a UDI-DI. PeriopUDI parses the GS1, HIBCC, or ICCBBA carrier in milliseconds.

02

Validate against FDA GUDID

We query the authoritative FDA registry live — manufacturer, GMDN type, MR compatibility, sterility, and any active recall.

03

Write a US Core FHIR Device

A conformant, structured, recall-aware Device resource lands in the patient's chart — ready for MRI screening, anesthesia review, and registry submission.
