Cardiac Rhythm Device Protocol Layer — UDI scan resolves to pacemaker/ICD → return NBG pacing code, pacer-dependence status, magnet behavior, electrocautery precautions, manufacturer 24-hr support line. Map GUDID make/model to HRS/ACC perioperative guidelines by device class.
Guideline-Backed Checklists — Every device class returns a structured "what do I do right now" checklist tied to ACC/AHA/HRS or ASA guidelines. UDI scan → actionable perioperative protocol, not a data sheet.
Institutional Override Layer — Admin-editable mechanism (JSON overlay is sufficient) for institution-specific annotations to sit on top of GUDID data, particularly for MRI compatibility where internal EP lists contradict manufacturer labeling.
Neuromodulation Module (VNS/DBS) — Pre-procedure mode guidance (off / specific mode / untouched) for vagus nerve stimulators and deep brain stimulators, scoped to surgery vs MRI vs electrophysiology study contexts.
Insulin Pump & CGM Module — Three sub-cases: standalone pump, standalone CGM, closed-loop hybrid. Priority is the closed-loop case (automated insulin delivery continuing silently under anesthesia) and the FDA electrocautery warning causing false CGM glucose readings.
